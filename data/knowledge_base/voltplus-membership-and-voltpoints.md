# VoltPlus Membership and VoltPoints Rewards

## VoltPlus membership

VoltPlus costs **$49 per year** and includes:

* Free Express shipping (1-2 business days) on every order.
* **60-day return window** instead of 30 days.
* No restocking fees and free return shipping.
* Double VoltPoints on every purchase.
* Priority support with a 1-hour response target during support hours.

You can cancel VoltPlus anytime. If you cancel within 14 days and haven't used any benefit, we refund the full fee.

## VoltPoints

* Earn **1 point per $1** spent (VoltPlus members earn **2 points per $1**).
* Every **100 points = $5** reward, applied at checkout.
* Points are credited when your order is delivered and removed if the item is returned.
* Points expire after **12 months without any account activity** (a purchase or a redemption).

Points have no cash value and cannot be transferred between accounts.
