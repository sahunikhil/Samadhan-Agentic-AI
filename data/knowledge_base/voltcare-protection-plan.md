# VoltCare+ Protection Plan

VoltCare+ extends your coverage beyond the standard warranty and adds **accidental damage protection**.

## What VoltCare+ includes

* Extends the standard warranty to a total of **3 years** from delivery.
* Covers accidental damage such as drops, cracked screens and liquid spills.
* Up to **2 accidental damage claims per 12 months**.
* Free express shipping for repairs in both directions.
* Battery replacement if capacity falls below 80% during the plan.

## Price and deductibles

| Product | Plan price | Deductible per accidental-damage claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Laptops | $199 | $49 |
| Phones and tablets | $99 | $29 |
| Audio and wearables | $39 | $15 |

Warranty (non-accidental) repairs under VoltCare+ have **no deductible**.

## Buying VoltCare+

VoltCare+ must be purchased within **60 days of the product's delivery date**. You can add it from your account page or ask support. The plan is transferable if you sell or gift the product.

## Cancelling

Cancel within 30 days of purchasing the plan for a full refund. After 30 days you receive a prorated refund, minus the value of any claims already paid.

## Not covered

Loss, theft, cosmetic damage that does not affect function, and intentional damage are not covered.
