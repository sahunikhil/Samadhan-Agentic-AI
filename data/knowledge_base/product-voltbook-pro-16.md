# VoltBook Pro 16 - Specifications and FAQ

The VoltBook Pro 16 is built for creators, developers and gamers. Price: **$1,899**.

## Specifications

* Display: 16-inch 3.2K mini-LED, 165Hz, 1,000 nits peak
* Memory and storage: 32GB DDR5 (2 slots, upgradeable to 64GB), 1TB NVMe SSD plus a free second M.2 slot
* Graphics: discrete GPU with 8GB GDDR7
* Battery: 99.9Wh, up to 12 hours of video playback
* Charging: **140W USB-C charger included**. USB-C chargers from 65W work but charge slowly and the laptop throttles performance when running on a charger below 100W.
* Ports: 2x Thunderbolt 5, 2x USB-A 3.2, HDMI 2.1, SD card reader (UHS-II), 2.5Gb Ethernet, 3.5mm jack
* Weight: 2.1 kg (4.6 lb)

## FAQ

**Can I take it on a plane?** Yes. The 99.9Wh battery is just under the 100Wh limit most airlines allow in carry-on luggage without approval.

**How many external displays?** Up to **three**: two via Thunderbolt 5 and one via HDMI.

**Does it get loud?** Choose *Silent*, *Balanced* or *Performance* modes in Volt Control Center (Fn + Q cycles modes).

**Is Linux supported?** Ubuntu 24.04 LTS and newer are certified.
