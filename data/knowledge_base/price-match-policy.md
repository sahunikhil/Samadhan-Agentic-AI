# Price Match and Price Adjustment Policy

## Price adjustment (we lowered our price)

If Voltwise lowers the price of an item within **14 days** of your purchase, we refund the difference. Ask support with your order number; the difference is refunded to your original payment method. Price adjustments do not apply to flash sales that last less than 24 hours.

## Competitor price match (at time of purchase)

We match the current price of an identical item (same model, color and storage) sold and shipped by a major authorized electronics retailer. Requirements:

* The item must be in stock at the competitor.
* The request must be made **before or at the time of purchase**. We don't match competitor prices after purchase.
* Marketplace or third-party sellers, clearance, open-box, refurbished, bundle deals and membership-only prices are excluded.

## Limits

Price matches are limited to 2 units per product per customer. We reserve the right to decline matches that appear to be pricing errors.
