# Damaged, Defective or Missing Items

## Damaged on arrival

If your item arrives damaged, report it within **7 days of delivery** with a photo of the item and the shipping box. We will send a free replacement or issue a full refund, including any shipping you paid. For damaged items worth **less than $25** you do not need to send the item back.

## Missing items

If your package arrived but an item is missing, check whether your order shipped in multiple boxes (your confirmation email lists each tracking number). If everything has been delivered and an item is still missing, contact us within **7 days** and we will reship it at no cost.

## Package marked delivered but not received

Wait **48 hours** - carriers sometimes mark packages as delivered early. Check with neighbors and your building's mail room. If it still hasn't arrived, contact us and we will open a carrier investigation. See the *Shipping Policy* for trace timelines.

## Defective products

A product that stops working because of a manufacturing defect is covered by the Voltwise warranty (see *Warranty Policy*). During the first **30 days** after delivery, a defective product can instead be returned for a full refund with free return shipping and no restocking fee.

## Wrong item received

If we sent the wrong item, we'll email a free return label and ship the correct item immediately, before we receive the wrong one back.
