# Contact Us and Support Hours

## Virtual assistant

Our AI assistant is available **24/7** and can check orders, track shipments, start returns, answer product and policy questions, and hand you to a human when needed.

## Human support

Human specialists are available:

* **Monday-Friday:** 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM Eastern Time
* **Saturday:** 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM Eastern Time
* **Sunday and U.S. federal holidays:** closed

When the assistant hands your case to a specialist, a support ticket is created automatically so you never have to repeat yourself.

## Response times

| Priority | First response target |
| --- | --- |
| Urgent (security, payment issues) | 1 business hour |
| High (orders not received, defective items) | 4 business hours |
| Normal | 1 business day |

VoltPlus members receive a 1-hour response target for all priorities during support hours.

## Mailing address for returns

Voltwise Returns, 4100 Logistics Way, Reno, NV 89502. Please use the prepaid label from your RMA email - packages without an RMA number may be delayed.
