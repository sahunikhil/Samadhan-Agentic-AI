# Nova 8 Smartphone - Specifications and FAQ

An unlocked 5G Android phone. Price: **$699** (128GB) or **$799** (256GB).

## Specifications

* Display: 6.4-inch AMOLED, 120Hz
* Battery: 5,000 mAh with **45W wired** and 15W wireless charging
* Cameras: 50MP main, 12MP ultra-wide, 10MP 3x telephoto
* Water and dust resistance: **IP68**
* Software: 5 years of Android OS upgrades and 6 years of security updates
* In the box: phone, USB-C to USB-C cable, SIM tool. **No charger is included.**

## FAQ

**Which charger do I need for 45W charging?** A USB-C PD charger that supports PPS at 45W or more, such as the **VoltCharge 65W**. Other chargers work at lower speeds.

**Is it unlocked?** Yes, it works with all major U.S. carriers and supports eSIM and one physical nano-SIM.

**Does it support expandable storage?** No, there is no microSD slot.
