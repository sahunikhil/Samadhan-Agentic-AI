# Shipping Policy

Voltwise ships from two fulfillment centers (Reno, NV and Columbus, OH). The center closest to your address is chosen automatically.

## Processing time

Orders placed before 2:00 PM local time on a business day are processed the same day. All other orders are processed within **1 business day**. While an order is in the `processing` status you can still change the shipping address or cancel it (see *Order Changes and Cancellations*).

## Domestic shipping options (United States)

| Method | Delivery time (after processing) | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3-5 business days | **Free on orders of $50 or more**, otherwise $5.99 |
| Express | 1-2 business days | $14.99 (free for VoltPlus members) |
| Next-Day | Next business day if ordered before 2:00 PM | $24.99 (select metro areas only) |

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays. Weekend delivery is not guaranteed for any method.

## Carriers and tracking

We ship with UPS, FedEx and USPS. You receive a tracking number by email as soon as the carrier scans your package. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first movement. You can also ask our assistant for the live status of any order.

## Delayed or stuck shipments

If tracking shows **no movement for 5 business days** on a domestic shipment, contact us and we open a trace with the carrier. If the carrier cannot locate the package within **5 business days after the trace is opened**, we ship a free replacement or issue a full refund, whichever you prefer. You never need to wait for the carrier's own claim process.

## Signature and high-value orders

Orders with a total above **$800** require an adult signature on delivery. If you will not be home, you can redirect the package to a UPS Access Point or FedEx location from the carrier's tracking page.

## P.O. boxes and APO/FPO

Standard shipping is available to P.O. boxes and APO/FPO/DPO addresses via USPS. Express and Next-Day are not available for these addresses.
