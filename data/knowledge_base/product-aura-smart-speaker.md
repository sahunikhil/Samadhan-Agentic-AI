# Aura Smart Speaker - Setup and FAQ

A Wi-Fi smart speaker with room-filling sound. Price: **$129**.

## Setup

1. Plug in the speaker and wait for the ring light to pulse orange.
2. Open the **Voltwise Home** app and tap **Add device**.
3. Choose your 2.4GHz or 5GHz Wi-Fi network. The speaker supports Wi-Fi 6.
4. The ring light turns solid white when setup is complete.

## Features

* Works with **Matter**, so it can be controlled from most smart home platforms.
* **Stereo pairing:** two Aura speakers can be paired as a left/right stereo pair in the app.
* Bluetooth 5.3 for playing directly from a phone without Wi-Fi.
* Microphone mute switch on the back (the ring light turns red when muted).

## Troubleshooting

**Setup fails or the light keeps pulsing orange.** Move the speaker closer to the router during setup and make sure your phone is on the same network. Networks that require a web login (hotels, dorms) are not supported.

**Factory reset.** Hold the volume-down and mute buttons for **12 seconds** until the ring light spins orange.
