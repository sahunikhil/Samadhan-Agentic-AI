# Pulse Buds Pro - Specifications and FAQ

True wireless earbuds with ANC. Price: **$179**.

## Specifications

* Battery: 8 hours per charge (6 hours with ANC), **32 hours total with the case**
* Charging case: USB-C and **Qi wireless charging**
* Water resistance: **IPX5** (sweat and rain; not for swimming)
* Bluetooth 5.4, multipoint, in-ear detection
* Three sizes of silicone ear tips included (S, M, L)

## FAQ

**Can I return opened earbuds?** Only if the **hygiene seal is intact**. For hygiene reasons, earbuds with a broken seal can only be returned if they are defective. Defective earbuds are covered by the 1-year warranty.

**How do I pair them?** Open the case with both earbuds inside and hold the button on the back of the case for **3 seconds** until the light pulses white.

**One earbud isn't working.** Put both earbuds in the case, close the lid for 15 seconds, then hold the case button for **15 seconds** until the light flashes amber to reset them. Then pair again.

**Can I buy a single replacement earbud?** Yes, replacement left or right earbuds are $69 each, and a replacement case is $59.
