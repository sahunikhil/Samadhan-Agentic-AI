# PowerCell 20K Power Bank - Specifications and FAQ

A 20,000 mAh USB-C power bank that can charge a laptop. Price: **$59**.

## Specifications

* Capacity: 20,000 mAh / **74Wh**
* Output: up to **65W** USB-C PD (enough for the VoltBook Air 14), plus one 18W USB-A port
* Recharge: 0-100% in about 2 hours with a 65W charger
* Weight: 430 g

## FAQ

**Can I bring it on a plane?** Yes. At **74Wh** it is below the 100Wh limit, so it is allowed in carry-on luggage on most airlines without approval. Power banks are never allowed in checked baggage.

**How many times can it charge my phone?** About 3.5 full charges of a Nova 8, or roughly one full charge of a VoltBook Air 14.

**Can it charge the VoltBook Pro 16?** It can extend battery life at 65W, but the Pro 16 will charge slowly and may still drain under heavy load.

**Warranty?** 1 year. Swollen or overheating power banks should be stopped immediately; contact support for a replacement.
