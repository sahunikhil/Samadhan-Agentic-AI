# Warranty Policy

Every Voltwise-branded product includes a limited warranty against manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.

## Coverage periods

| Product type | Warranty |
| --- | --- |
| Laptops (VoltBook Air 14, VoltBook Pro 16) | **2 years** |
| Phones, tablets, headphones, earbuds, speakers, watches | **1 year** |
| Chargers, docks, cables and power banks | **1 year** |
| Built-in batteries | 1 year, or until capacity drops below **80%**, whichever comes first |

The warranty starts on the delivery date and is not extended by repairs or replacements.

## What is not covered

* Accidental damage (drops, cracked screens, liquid spills) - see *VoltCare+ Protection Plan*.
* Water damage beyond the product's rated water resistance.
* Damage from unauthorized repairs or modifications.
* Normal wear such as scratches or worn ear cushions.
* Lost or stolen products.

## How to make a claim

1. Contact support. We will run a short troubleshooting session first - many issues are fixed without a repair.
2. If the product is defective, we create a warranty RMA and email a free shipping label.
3. We repair or replace the product within **10 business days** of receiving it. If the same model is no longer available, we replace it with an equivalent or better model.

Proof of purchase is your Voltwise order number. Products bought from other retailers need a receipt.

## Warranty versus returns

During your return window you can simply return a defective product for a refund. After the return window, defective products are handled as warranty repairs or replacements, not refunds.
