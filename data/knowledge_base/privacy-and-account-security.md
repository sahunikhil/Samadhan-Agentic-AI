# Privacy and Account Security

## Protecting your account

* Use a unique password and turn on two-factor authentication under **Account > Security**.
* Voltwise will **never ask for your password, full card number or one-time codes** in chat, email or by phone.
* Our support assistant can only see orders that belong to the signed-in account.

## Your data rights

You can download a copy of your data or request deletion from **Account > Privacy**. Deletion requests are completed within **30 days**. Order records required for tax and warranty purposes are retained for 7 years and then deleted.

## Chat transcripts

Support chats are stored for **90 days** to help resolve follow-up questions and improve our service, then deleted. Card numbers accidentally typed into chat are automatically masked.

## Suspicious activity

If you notice an order you didn't place, contact support immediately. We will lock the account, cancel any unshipped orders and help you reset your credentials.
