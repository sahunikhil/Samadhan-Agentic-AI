# International Shipping

Voltwise ships to **Canada, the United Kingdom, all European Union countries and Australia**. We do not currently ship to other destinations.

## Delivery times and cost

International orders are delivered in **7-14 business days** after processing. Shipping is a flat **$19.99** per order, or free on orders of **$150 or more**. Express and Next-Day options are not available internationally.

## Duties and taxes

* **UK and EU:** import VAT and duties are calculated and collected at checkout (Delivered Duty Paid). Nothing is due on delivery.
* **Canada and Australia:** duties and taxes are *not* included and are collected by the carrier on delivery (Delivered Duty Unpaid).

Refused international packages are returned to us and refunded minus the original shipping cost and any return charges billed by the carrier.

## Voltage and plugs

All Voltwise chargers are universal (100-240V). International orders include the correct wall plug for the destination country.

## Returns from outside the U.S.

The same return windows apply (see *Returns and Refunds Policy*), but international customers pay for return shipping unless the item is defective or we sent the wrong item. We recommend a tracked service because we cannot refund a return that never arrives.

## Batteries and air freight

Standalone lithium batteries above 100Wh cannot ship internationally. The PowerCell 20K power bank (74Wh) ships to all supported countries.
