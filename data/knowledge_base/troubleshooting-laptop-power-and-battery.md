# Troubleshooting Laptop Power, Charging and Battery

## Laptop won't turn on

1. Connect the original charger and wait **15 minutes** - a fully drained battery needs time before the laptop can boot.
2. Check the charging LED next to the USB-C port: **amber = charging, white = full, off = no power**.
3. Perform a **hard reset**: unplug everything and hold the power button for **15 seconds**, then press it normally.
4. Try a different outlet and a different USB-C port.

If the charging LED stays off with a known-good charger, the laptop needs warranty service.

## Battery drains quickly

* Update BIOS and drivers with **Volt Update** (Start menu > Volt Update). BIOS 1.08 improved standby drain on the VoltBook Air 14.
* Lower the display refresh rate to 60Hz on battery (Settings > Display).
* Use *Silent* mode in Volt Control Center.
* Check **Settings > System > Power > Battery usage** for apps using power in the background.

A healthy battery keeps at least 80% of its original capacity for 500 charge cycles. Check battery health in Volt Control Center > Battery. Batteries below 80% within the first year are replaced under warranty.

## Charging slowly or "plugged in, not charging"

* Use a charger of at least 45W (Air 14) or 100W (Pro 16) and a 5A-rated cable.
* **Battery Care mode** stops charging at 80% to extend battery life. Turn it off in Volt Control Center if you need a full charge.
* Charging pauses if the laptop is too hot. Let it cool and remove it from soft surfaces like beds.
