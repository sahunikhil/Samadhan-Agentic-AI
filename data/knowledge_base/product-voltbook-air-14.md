# VoltBook Air 14 - Specifications and FAQ

The VoltBook Air 14 is our thin-and-light laptop for everyday work and study. Price: **$1,099**.

## Specifications

* Display: 14-inch 2.8K OLED, 120Hz, 500 nits
* Memory and storage: 16GB LPDDR5X RAM (not upgradeable), 512GB NVMe SSD (user-replaceable M.2 2280)
* Battery: 72Wh, **up to 18 hours** of video playback
* Charging: USB-C Power Delivery, **65W charger included**; 30 minutes of charging gives about 50% battery
* Ports: 2x USB4 (USB-C, 40Gbps, DisplayPort 1.4 and charging), 1x USB-A 3.2, 1x HDMI 2.1, 3.5mm headphone jack
* Wireless: Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.4
* Weight: 1.2 kg (2.65 lb)
* Operating system: Windows 11 Home

## FAQ

**Can I charge it with a phone charger?** Yes, any USB-C PD charger works, but chargers under 45W charge slowly and may not keep up during heavy use. See the *Charger Compatibility Guide*.

**Can it drive external monitors?** Yes, up to **two external 4K displays**: one via HDMI 2.1 and one via USB4, or two via a USB4 dock.

**Can I upgrade the RAM?** No. The RAM is soldered. The SSD can be upgraded to up to 4TB.

**Is there a touchscreen?** The standard model is not touch. The VB-AIR-14T variant adds touch and pen support for $100 more.

**Warranty?** 2 years, like all Voltwise laptops. Opened laptops returned within the return window are subject to a 15% restocking fee unless defective or you are a VoltPlus member.
