# Pulse ANC Headphones - Specifications and FAQ

Over-ear wireless headphones with adaptive active noise cancellation (ANC). Price: **$249**.

## Specifications

* Battery: **40 hours with ANC off, 30 hours with ANC on**
* Fast charge: **10 minutes of USB-C charging gives 5 hours** of playback
* Bluetooth 5.4 with **multipoint** (connected to two devices at once)
* Codecs: AAC, SBC, LDAC
* Wired listening with the included 3.5mm cable (works even with a dead battery, ANC off)
* Weight: 250 g

## FAQ

**How do I enter pairing mode?** With the headphones off, press and hold the power button for **5 seconds** until the LED flashes blue.

**How do I reset them?** Hold the **power and ANC buttons together for 10 seconds** until the LED flashes red three times. This clears all paired devices.

**Can I return them after opening?** Yes. Over-ear headphones are not subject to the hygiene-seal rule that applies to earbuds, so opened Pulse ANC headphones can be returned within the normal return window if undamaged.

**Are the ear cushions replaceable?** Yes. Replacement cushions (PULSE-CUSH) cost $29. Worn cushions are considered normal wear and are not covered by warranty.
