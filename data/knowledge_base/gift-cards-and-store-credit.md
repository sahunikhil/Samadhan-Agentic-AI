# Gift Cards and Store Credit

## Gift cards

* Voltwise gift cards are available from $25 to $500 and **never expire**.
* Gift cards are **non-refundable** and cannot be exchanged for cash, except where required by law.
* Gift cards cannot be used to buy other gift cards.
* Lost or stolen gift cards can be replaced only if you have the original order number and the card has not been redeemed.

## Store credit

Store credit is issued for returns when you choose it instead of a refund, and includes a **10% bonus**. Store credit:

* is added to your account instantly when the carrier scans your return,
* never expires,
* can be combined with other payment methods at checkout,
* cannot be converted back to a refund to your card.

Check your balance on **Account > Wallet** or ask our assistant.
