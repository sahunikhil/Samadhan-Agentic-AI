# Returns and Refunds Policy

We want you to love your purchase. If you don't, most items can be returned for a refund, store credit or exchange.

## Return window

* **Standard customers:** 30 days from the delivery date.
* **VoltPlus members:** 60 days from the delivery date.

The window is counted from the delivery date shown in tracking, not the order date. Holiday purchases made between November 1 and December 24 can be returned until January 31 of the following year.

## Condition requirements

Items must include all original accessories, manuals and packaging. Opened items are accepted as long as they are undamaged, with these exceptions:

* **In-ear headphones and earbuds** (including Pulse Buds Pro) can only be returned if the **hygiene seal is intact**, unless the product is defective.
* **Non-returnable items:** gift cards, downloadable software and licenses, and any item marked *Final Sale*.

## Restocking fee

A **15% restocking fee** applies to *opened* laptops and tablets. The fee is **waived** if the item is defective, if we shipped the wrong item, or if you are a VoltPlus member. Unopened laptops and tablets have no restocking fee.

## Return shipping

We email a prepaid return label. The label is free if the item is defective or we made a mistake; otherwise **$6.99** is deducted from your refund. Return shipping is always free for VoltPlus members.

## Refund timing and method

Once our warehouse receives your return, we inspect it and process the refund within **3 business days**. Your bank may take an additional **5-7 business days** to post the credit to your original payment method.

You can choose **store credit** instead: it is issued instantly when the return is scanned by the carrier and includes a **10% bonus** (for example, a $100 return becomes $110 in credit).

## Exchanges

To exchange for a different color or size of the same product, request an exchange. We ship the replacement as soon as the carrier scans your return package, so you are never without your device for long.

## Refund approvals

Refunds up to $100 are approved automatically. Larger refunds are reviewed by a support specialist, usually within a few business hours.
