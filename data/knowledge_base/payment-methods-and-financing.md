# Payment Methods and Financing

## Accepted payment methods

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Voltwise gift cards and store credit.

## When you are charged

We place a temporary **authorization hold** when you order and capture the payment **when the order ships**. If an order is cancelled before shipping, the hold is released by your bank within 3-5 business days.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, the order is held for **48 hours** while you update the payment method from your account. Orders still unpaid after 48 hours are cancelled automatically.

## Financing with Affirm

Orders of **$300 or more** qualify for Affirm financing. Choose 6 or 12 monthly payments at **0% APR** for eligible customers, or 24 months with rates from 10-30% APR, subject to credit approval. Returns of financed purchases are refunded to Affirm, which adjusts your loan.

## Security

Voltwise support will **never ask for your full card number, CVV or account password** by chat, email or phone. If someone asks, do not share it and report it to security@voltwise.example.

## Invoices

A VAT/tax invoice is emailed when your order ships. You can also download invoices from **Account > Orders**.
