# Charger Compatibility Guide

All Voltwise chargers use USB-C Power Delivery (PD) and work worldwide (100-240V).

## VoltCharge chargers

| Charger | Price | Ports | Max output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VoltCharge 65W (VC-65W) | $49 | 2x USB-C, 1x USB-A | 65W from one port; shared when multiple devices are connected |
| VoltCharge 140W (VC-140W) | $89 | 3x USB-C | 140W from one port |

## Which charger for which device?

| Device | Recommended | Also works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VoltBook Air 14 | VoltCharge 65W (included in the box) | Any USB-C PD charger of 45W or more |
| VoltBook Pro 16 | VoltCharge 140W (included in the box) | 100W+ for full performance; 65W charges slowly and throttles performance |
| Nova 8 | VoltCharge 65W (PPS for full 45W speed) | Any USB-C PD charger |
| Pulse ANC, Pulse Buds Pro, Aura | Any USB-C charger | - |

## Sharing power between devices

When the VoltCharge 65W charges two devices at once, it splits power: 45W + 20W across two USB-C ports. A VoltBook Air 14 still charges at 45W in this mode, which is enough for normal use.

## Cables

Charging above 60W requires a **5A (100W or 240W) rated USB-C cable**. The cables included with VoltBook laptops are 5A rated. Phone cables are usually 3A and limit charging to 60W.
