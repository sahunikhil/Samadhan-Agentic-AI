# Troubleshooting Bluetooth Pairing and Audio Problems

Work through these steps in order. Most connection problems are fixed by step 3.

1. **Charge the device** for at least 15 minutes. Low battery causes random disconnects.
2. **Remove the device** from your phone or computer's Bluetooth list ("Forget this device"), then turn Bluetooth off and on.
3. **Reset the headphones or earbuds:**
   * Pulse ANC: hold power + ANC for 10 seconds (LED flashes red three times).
   * Pulse Buds Pro: with both earbuds in the case, hold the case button for 15 seconds (light flashes amber).
4. **Put the device back into pairing mode** and pair again.
5. **Update the firmware** in the Voltwise Audio app (Settings > Firmware). Firmware 3.2 fixed multipoint dropouts on Windows laptops.

## Audio cuts out or stutters

* Keep your phone within 10 meters (33 ft) without walls in between.
* Turn off LDAC (Settings > Sound quality) - high-resolution mode needs a strong signal.
* Microwaves and crowded 2.4GHz Wi-Fi can interfere with Bluetooth.

## Microphone sounds muffled on calls

Make sure the device is used for calls as a "headset" rather than "headphones" in your computer's sound settings, and that ANC mic openings are not covered.

## Still not working?

If these steps don't help, the device may be defective. Contact support - within the return window we can refund or replace it; afterwards it is handled under warranty.
